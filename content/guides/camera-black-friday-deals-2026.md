## How to spot a fake Black Friday deal

- **Check the price history.** Every camera's page has a chart of its lowest price. If the "deal" price is the same as last month's, it isn't a deal.
- **Compare against the launch price, not the "was" price.** A store's "was" price can be anything. The maker's launch price is a fixed reference, and we show it on each product page as the RRP.
- **Watch for kits.** A "body + lens" bundle can look cheap until you price the lens on its own. Our headline prices are always body only.
- **Grey imports.** A price far below everyone else may be a grey import with no local warranty. Read [grey market cameras](/guides/grey-market-cameras) before you buy one.
- **Maker promotions end.** Many US Black Friday prices are maker instant savings with a fixed end date. [When camera prices drop](/guides/when-camera-prices-drop) explains how they work.
