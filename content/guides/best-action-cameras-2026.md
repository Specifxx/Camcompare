An action camera is the one you can drop, dunk and strap to a helmet. In 2026 the big three (DJI, GoPro and Insta360) are closer than ever, and the differences are in sensor size, waterproofing and whether you want a 360 camera instead. Here is how they compare, with today's prices.

## Our picks

[[picks:dji-osmo-action-6,gopro-hero13-black,gopro-mission-1-pro,insta360-x5,insta360-x6]]

- **Best overall: DJI Osmo Action 6.** A bigger sensor than most action cameras means cleaner footage at dusk and indoors, and it is waterproof to 20 m without a case.
- **Best value: GoPro Hero 13 Black.** The most accessories of any action camera, and often discounted.
- **Best image quality: GoPro Mission 1 Pro.** A 1-inch sensor, 8K and 4K up to 240p, for when an action camera has to look like a "real" one.
- **Best 360 camera: Insta360 X5.** Film everything around you and choose the angle later. Its newer sibling, the **X6**, adds a bigger sensor.

## Action camera or 360 camera?

A regular action camera points one way, like any camera. A **360 camera** records in every direction at once, so you can reframe after the fact and the selfie stick disappears from the shot. The trade-off is more editing and lower sharpness once you crop to a normal frame. If you film sport from a helmet or a bike, a regular action camera is simpler. If you film travel, skiing or anything where you don't know where the action will be, 360 is magic.

## Tips

- **Older models are a bargain.** When a new GoPro or DJI arrives, last year's model often drops a lot. Each camera's page shows its price history.
- **Batteries.** Cold weather drains them fast. A spare is the best accessory you can buy.
- **Memory cards.** 4K at high frame rates needs a fast card. Look for V30 or faster.
