A travel camera has one job a phone can't quite do: come home with pictures you would print, without being the thing you resent carrying by day three. That rules out most big bodies and big lenses. Here is what we would pack, at every budget, with today's prices from the stores we track.

## Our picks

[[picks:panasonic-lumix-zs99,dji-osmo-pocket-3,canon-eos-r50,fujifilm-x-s20,om-system-om-5-mark-ii,sony-a7c-ii]]

- **Best pocket zoom: Panasonic Lumix ZS99.** A long 24–720mm-equivalent zoom in a body that fits a jacket pocket. Best in daylight; the small sensor struggles indoors at night.
- **Best for video: DJI Osmo Pocket 3.** A 1-inch sensor on a built-in gimbal, so walking shots come out smooth without a rig. It is a video camera first; stills are a bonus.
- **Best light interchangeable-lens camera: Canon EOS R50.** Small, simple and cheap with its kit lens. It has no in-body stabilisation, so pick a stabilised lens for low light.
- **Best all-rounder: Fujifilm X-S20.** APS-C with in-body stabilisation, a proper grip and good battery life for a small body. Fujifilm's film looks save a lot of editing on the road.
- **Best for bad weather: OM System OM-5 Mark II.** Weather sealed, light, and the Micro Four Thirds lenses are the smallest of any system, so a two-lens kit still fits a day bag.
- **Best image quality you can still carry: Sony a7C II.** Full frame in a rangefinder-style body. Pair it with a small zoom or a pancake prime and it is barely bigger than an APS-C camera.

## How to choose

- **One device, no lens changes:** a pocket zoom like the ZS99, or a 1-inch compact. See [the best compact cameras of 2026](/guides/best-compact-cameras-2026).
- **You mostly film:** the Pocket 3, or an action camera if you swim or ride. See [the best action cameras of 2026](/guides/best-action-cameras-2026) and [the best vlogging cameras](/guides/best-vlogging-cameras-2026).
- **You want better pictures than your phone in every light:** an interchangeable-lens body with one travel zoom. APS-C or Micro Four Thirds keeps the lenses small; full frame costs you weight mostly in the lenses, not the body. Our [sensor sizes explainer](/guides/sensor-sizes-explained) and [full frame vs APS-C](/guides/full-frame-vs-aps-c) go deeper.
- **Your budget is tight:** a used body is the best value in cameras. Read [buying used cameras](/guides/buying-used-cameras) before you do.

## Before you fly

Pack a spare battery (the airline rules allow camera batteries in cabin baggage only), a second memory card, and check that the charger takes USB-C so one cable covers the camera and your phone.
